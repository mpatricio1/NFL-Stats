The offensive line allowed a 19% pressure rate (13th of 32) but committed 37 accepted penalties for 249 yards, ranked 32nd of 32 for penalties per game. Scheme note: play action from under center was used on just 2% of dropbacks (31st).

J.J. Watt led the defense with 12.5 sacks, 31 pressures and 25 QB hits, and Zach Allen added 5.5 sacks and 25 pressures. In coverage, Marco Wilson allowed a 77.1 rating on 87 targets, but Byron Murphy allowed 103.1 and the team passer rating allowed was 99.8, ranked 29th of 32. The Edge/DL group allowed 107.0 (28th). On special teams, Matt Prater made 22 of 25 field goals and Andy Lee averaged 40.9 net yards on 67 punts.
