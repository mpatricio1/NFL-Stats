Regular season through Week 5, data as of 2026-10-14. The interactive page is at https://mpatricio1.github.io/NFL-Stats/; this page and the files below hold the same numbers without needing JavaScript. Rebuilt every Wednesday.

Sources: nflverse (play-by-play EPA, Next Gen Stats, FTN charting, PFR advanced stats, snap counts) and FTN's weekly DVOA ratings. Column notes: README.md.

Files

  • data.json: The full dataset the page is drawn from (every file listed here, plus team colors and per-team detail)
  • team_summary.csv: One row per team: record, points, EPA/play and success rate (offense, defense, pass, rush) with ranks, FTN DVOA, broken tackles, pressure, coverage allowed, FTN charting rates, average personnel on the field, accepted penalties, presnap motion (32 rows)
  • dvoa.csv: FTN DVOA ratings through Week 5 (total, offense, defense, special teams, with ranks) (32 rows)
  • team_efficiency.csv: Team EPA, success rate, points and ranks (also inside team_summary.csv) (32 rows)
  • qbs.csv: Quarterbacks: EPA per dropback, CPOE, success rate, sack rate, NGS time to throw and aggressiveness (q = meets NGS qualifier) (43 rows)
  • rushers.csv: Rushers league-wide: carries, yards, EPA per carry, success rate, NGS rush yards over expected (68 rows)
  • receivers_ngs.csv: Receivers and TEs league-wide (NGS qualifiers): targets, separation, YAC over expected, intended air yards (118 rows)
  • qb_pressure_to_sack.csv: QB pressures, sacks and pressure-to-sack rate (PFR) (43 rows)
  • broken_tackles_team.csv: Broken tackles by team (PFR, rushing + receiving), per game and rank (32 rows)
  • broken_tackles_leaders.csv: Broken tackle leaders (PFR) (10 rows)
  • personnel_avg_on_field.csv: Average players on the field per snap by position (from snap counts) (32 rows)
  • motion_team.csv: Presnap motion by team (FTN is_motion + pbp EPA): motion rate and EPA/play, yards/play, success rate with (_m) and without (_n) motion, all plays / pass_ / run_, offense (o_) and defense faced (d_), with ranks (32 rows)
  • penalties_team.csv: Accepted penalties by team: count and yards, split offense/defense/special teams, first downs given by defensive fouls; per game and rank (1 = fewest) (32 rows)
  • ftn_offense.csv: FTN charting rates, offense: play action, motion, interceptable throws, drops, blitzes faced, out of pocket, screens (32 rows)
  • ftn_defense.csv: FTN charting rates, defense (same columns, as faced/allowed) (32 rows)
  • team_games.csv: Game log: opponent, score, EPA, success rate, yards, turnovers (all 32 teams, team column) (158 rows)
  • team_pa_split.csv: Play action vs. not: EPA per dropback (all 32 teams, team column) (64 rows)
  • team_blitz.csv: Blitzed vs. not: EPA per dropback (all 32 teams, team column) (64 rows)
  • team_qb_ngs.csv: NGS passing by week (week 0 = season) (all 32 teams, team column) (198 rows)
  • team_rushers.csv: Team rushers: carries, yards, EPA, success rate, RYOE (all 32 teams, team column) (108 rows)
  • team_rush_pfr.csv: Yards before and after contact (PFR) (all 32 teams, team column) (214 rows)
  • team_bt_players.csv: Broken tackles by player (PFR) (all 32 teams, team column) (453 rows)
  • team_lanes.csv: Run results by gap/lane (all 32 teams, team column) (240 rows)
  • team_rec.csv: Receiving: targets, share, EPA/target, separation, YAC over expected (all 32 teams, team column) (347 rows)
  • team_snaps.csv: Snap counts and shares, offense and defense (all 32 teams, team column) (1818 rows)
  • team_defense.csv: Defenders: snaps, coverage allowed, pressures, missed tackles, tackles, sacks (all 32 teams, team column) (697 rows)
  • team_st.csv: Special teams: kicking, punting, returns (all 32 teams, team column) (176 rows)
  • team_sacks.csv: Every sack taken, with FTN context (rushers, blitzers, QB fault) and bucket (all 32 teams, team column) (357 rows)
  • team_backs.csv: Offense by backs in the backfield (FTN), with league comparison (all 32 teams, team column) (96 rows)
  • team_box_off.csv: Designed runs by defenders in the box (FTN), with league comparison (all 32 teams, team column) (96 rows)
  • team_box_def.csv: Run defense by box count (FTN), with league comparison (all 32 teams, team column) (96 rows)
  • team_rush_def.csv: Pass defense by number of pass rushers (FTN), with league comparison (all 32 teams, team column) (96 rows)
  • team_penalty_players.csv: Accepted penalties by player and page section (QB, RB, WR incl. TE, OL, FRONT, DB; ST = any flag on a kick or punt) (all 32 teams, team column) (710 rows)
  • team_penalties_by_group.csv: Accepted penalties by page section: flags, yards and per-game rank among 32 teams, 1 = fewest (TEAM = no player named) (all 32 teams, team column) (288 rows)
  • penalties.csv: Every accepted foul: game, week, team, phase (off/def/st), type, yards, player, roster position, page section (1192 rows)
  • team_coverage_by_group.csv: Coverage allowed by position group (CB, S, LB, Edge/DL), PFR (all 32 teams, team column) (130 rows)
  • commentary.csv: Hand-written team write-ups, one row per team and section (416 rows)
  • commentary.md: The same write-ups as one readable Markdown file (416 rows)
