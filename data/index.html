Regular season through Week 6, data as of 2026-10-20. The interactive page is at https://mpatricio1.github.io/NFL-Stats/; this page and the files below hold the same numbers without needing JavaScript. Rebuilt every Wednesday.

Sources: nflverse (play-by-play EPA, Next Gen Stats, FTN charting, PFR advanced stats, snap counts) and FTN's weekly DVOA ratings. Column notes: README.md.

Files

  • data.json: The full dataset the page is drawn from (every file listed here, plus team colors and per-team detail)
  • team_summary.csv: One row per team: record, points, EPA/play and success rate (offense, defense, pass, rush) with ranks, FTN DVOA, broken tackles, pressure, coverage allowed, FTN charting rates, average personnel on the field, accepted penalties, presnap motion, play action by QB alignment, heavy sets (32 rows)
  • dvoa.csv: FTN DVOA ratings through Week 6 (total, offense, defense, special teams, with ranks) (32 rows)
  • team_efficiency.csv: Team EPA, success rate, points and ranks (also inside team_summary.csv) (32 rows)
  • qbs.csv: Quarterbacks: EPA per dropback, CPOE, success rate, sack rate, NGS time to throw and aggressiveness (q = meets NGS qualifier) (45 rows)
  • rushers.csv: Rushers league-wide: carries, yards, EPA per carry, success rate, NGS rush yards over expected (74 rows)
  • receivers_ngs.csv: Receivers and TEs league-wide (NGS qualifiers): targets, separation, YAC over expected, intended air yards (125 rows)
  • qb_pressure_to_sack.csv: QB pressures, sacks and pressure-to-sack rate (PFR) (43 rows)
  • broken_tackles_team.csv: Broken tackles by team (PFR, rushing + receiving), per game and rank (32 rows)
  • broken_tackles_leaders.csv: Broken tackle leaders (PFR) (10 rows)
  • personnel_avg_on_field.csv: Average players on the field per snap by position (from snap counts) (32 rows)
  • motion_team.csv: Presnap motion by team (FTN is_motion + pbp EPA): motion rate and EPA/play, yards/play, success rate with (_m) and without (_n) motion, all plays / pass_ / run_, offense (o_) and defense faced (d_), with ranks (32 rows)
  • pa_under_center_team.csv: Play action from under center by team (FTN is_play_action + qb_location U, pbp EPA): rate = share of all dropbacks, arate = share of under-center dropbacks; EPA/play, yards/play, success rate on those plays (_m) and on all dropbacks without play action (_n); offense (o_) and defense faced (d_), with ranks (32 rows)
  • pa_shotgun_team.csv: Play action from shotgun by team (FTN qb_location S; pistol is in formation_splits_team.csv), same columns as pa_under_center_team.csv (32 rows)
  • formation_splits_team.csv: Plays by QB alignment (kind qb: U under center, S shotgun, P pistol) and backfield (kind bf: 0, 1, 2, 3+ players besides the QB, jumbo = extra lineman reported eligible) for each team, offense (side o) and defense faced (side d), team LG = league: plays, share, EPA/play, success rate, dropbacks and runs with EPA, play action count, share and EPA with/without (520 rows)
  • heavy_sets_team.csv: Heavy sets by team: plays with 2+ players in the backfield besides the QB (FTN n_offense_backfield) or an extra lineman reported eligible (jumbo; a proxy, since per-play personnel isn't published for 2026), same columns as motion_team.csv (32 rows)
  • penalties_team.csv: Accepted penalties by team: count and yards, split offense/defense/special teams, first downs given by defensive fouls; per game and rank (1 = fewest) (32 rows)
  • ftn_offense.csv: FTN charting rates, offense: play action, motion, interceptable throws, drops, blitzes faced, out of pocket, screens (32 rows)
  • ftn_defense.csv: FTN charting rates, defense (same columns, as faced/allowed) (32 rows)
  • team_games.csv: Game log: opponent, score, EPA, success rate, yards, turnovers (all 32 teams, team column) (186 rows)
  • team_pa_split.csv: Play action vs. not: EPA per dropback (all 32 teams, team column) (64 rows)
  • team_blitz.csv: Blitzed vs. not: EPA per dropback (all 32 teams, team column) (64 rows)
  • team_qb_ngs.csv: NGS passing by week (week 0 = season) (all 32 teams, team column) (229 rows)
  • team_rushers.csv: Team rushers: carries, yards, EPA, success rate, RYOE (all 32 teams, team column) (120 rows)
  • team_rush_pfr.csv: Yards before and after contact (PFR) (all 32 teams, team column) (214 rows)
  • team_bt_players.csv: Broken tackles by player (PFR) (all 32 teams, team column) (454 rows)
  • team_lanes.csv: Run results by gap/lane (all 32 teams, team column) (242 rows)
  • team_rec.csv: Receiving: targets, share, EPA/target, separation, YAC over expected (all 32 teams, team column) (363 rows)
  • team_snaps.csv: Snap counts and shares, offense and defense (all 32 teams, team column) (1866 rows)
  • team_defense.csv: Defenders: snaps, coverage allowed, pressures, missed tackles, tackles, sacks (all 32 teams, team column) (720 rows)
  • team_st.csv: Special teams: kicking, punting, returns (all 32 teams, team column) (187 rows)
  • team_sacks.csv: Every sack taken, with FTN context (rushers, blitzers, QB fault) and bucket (all 32 teams, team column) (423 rows)
  • team_backs.csv: Offense by backs in the backfield (FTN), with league comparison (all 32 teams, team column) (96 rows)
  • team_box_off.csv: Designed runs by defenders in the box (FTN), with league comparison (all 32 teams, team column) (96 rows)
  • team_box_def.csv: Run defense by box count (FTN), with league comparison (all 32 teams, team column) (96 rows)
  • team_rush_def.csv: Pass defense by number of pass rushers (FTN), with league comparison (all 32 teams, team column) (96 rows)
  • team_penalty_players.csv: Accepted penalties by player and page section (QB, RB, WR incl. TE, OL, FRONT, DB; ST = any flag on a kick or punt) (all 32 teams, team column) (784 rows)
  • team_penalties_by_group.csv: Accepted penalties by page section: flags, yards and per-game rank among 32 teams, 1 = fewest (TEAM = no player named) (all 32 teams, team column) (288 rows)
  • penalties.csv: Every accepted foul: game, week, team, phase (off/def/st), type, yards, player, roster position, page section (1400 rows)
  • team_coverage_by_group.csv: Coverage allowed by position group (CB, S, LB, Edge/DL), PFR (all 32 teams, team column) (130 rows)
  • commentary.csv: Hand-written team write-ups, one row per team and section (512 rows)
  • commentary.md: The same write-ups as one readable Markdown file (512 rows)
